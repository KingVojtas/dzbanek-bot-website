    A simulated channel that runs in your browser. Type / to see the commands, or click one below. The buttons on the cards work too.

    / opens the list, ↑ ↓ choose, Enter sends, Esc closes.

    Slash commands

    /play

    Joins your voice channel and plays a YouTube or Spotify link or a search. play_next puts the song next in the queue.

    • play_next
    /queue

    Shows the upcoming songs, one page at a time.

    /stop

    Stops playback, clears the queue, deletes the Now Playing card, and leaves the channel.

    /remove

    Removes one song from the queue. Position 1 is the next song.

    /radio

    play, stop, night, and night-off. The stations are Kiss, Rock, and Beat.

    Buttons on the cards

    Now Playing

    Pause
    Pauses, or resumes if paused.
    Skip
    Skips to the next song.
    Stop
    Stops and leaves. On the radio card you need to be in the bot’s voice channel.
    Loop
    Switches between Loop (off), Repeat (this song), and Queue (the whole queue).
    Shuffle
    Shuffles the upcoming songs.

    Radio

    Website ↗
    Opens the station’s website.
    Catch
    Saves the artist and title for you. It doesn’t work while a show is on, only for songs.
    Play
    Appears on a caught song. Finds it on YouTube and queues it like /play. On the Kitchen Board, only the person who caught the song can press it.

    /queue

    ◀ Prev
    Previous page of the queue.

    Next page of the queue.
