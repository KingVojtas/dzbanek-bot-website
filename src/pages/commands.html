    Music 8

    /play

    Joins your voice channel and plays a YouTube or Spotify link, or whatever you search for. play_next puts it at the front of the queue.

    • play_next
    /queue

    Shows what’s coming up, one page at a time.

    • ◀ Prev
    • Next ▶
    /nowplaying

    Brings the Now Playing card down to the bottom of the chat, so the buttons are right there.

    /skip

    Skips to the next song.

    /pause

    Pauses the music, or resumes it if it’s already paused.

    /volume

    Sets the volume for everyone in the channel, from 25 to 150. 100 is normal.

    • percent
    /remove

    Removes one song from the queue. Position 1 is the next song.

    /stop

    Stops the music, clears the queue, deletes the Now Playing card, and leaves.

    Radio 5

    /radio

    Seven live stations and Kitchen FM, plus Friday’s Radio Night.

    play station
    Joins your voice channel and starts one of the seven stations, or Kitchen FM.
    stop
    Stops the stream, deletes the radio card, and leaves.
    night station channel Manage Server
    Puts Radio Night on the calendar: a vote on Friday from 12:00, and the winner at 20:00.
    night-off Manage Server
    Takes Radio Night off the calendar.
    dedicate message to song New
    Sends Kitchen FM’s DJ up to 120 characters to read on air, before the next song or one you name.

    Steam wishlist 3

    /steam

    Wishlist Radar: get pinged when a game on your Steam wishlist goes on sale. The wishlist has to be public.

    link profile
    Links your Steam profile: a link, your custom name, or the 17-digit ID.
    wishlist
    Your whole wishlist with prices, sales first, ten games a page. Only you see it.
    unlink
    Stops watching your wishlist and deletes the link.

    Kitchen Quiz 2

    /quiz

    Name that tune in voice. It plays 15-second clips of songs your kitchen has played, or of a playlist you give it, and everyone picks the title from four buttons.

    start rounds playlist
    Starts a quiz in your voice channel: 5 to 20 rounds, 10 unless you say otherwise.
    stop
    Ends it now and shows the podium.

    Help 1

    /help

    Shows every command on one card. Add a command’s name to get just that one, with all its options.

    • command

    No command by that name.

    Music and radio share one voice connection per server, so starting one stops the other.

    What the buttons do

    Now Playing

    ⏸️ Pause
    Pauses the song. While it’s paused, the same button says ▶️ Resume.
    ⏭️ Skip
    Skips to the next song.
    ⏹️ Stop
    Stops, clears the queue, and leaves. The radio card has one too.
    🔁 Loop
    Switches between Loop (off), Repeat (this song), and Queue (the whole queue).
    🔀 Shuffle
    Shuffles what’s left in the queue.
    ⏮️ Previous
    Goes back to the song before this one.
    🔉 🔊
    Turns the volume down or up for everyone.
    📜 Queue
    Shows what’s coming up, only to you. Anyone can press it; the other buttons need you in the bot’s voice channel.

    Kitchen FM

    🔥 4
    Keeps a new find in the kitchen and brings a kitchen song back more often. The number is how many 🔥 the song has.
    👎
    Drops a new find for good and rests a song people are tired of. One of the two per person per song; press it again to take it back.

    Radio

    🌐 Website
    Opens the station’s website.
    🍪 Catch
    Saves the artist and title for you. Works on songs, not on talk shows.
    ▶️ Play
    Shows up on a song you caught. Finds it on YouTube and queues it like /play. On the Kitchen Board, only the person who caught it can press it.
    💋 Kiss · 2
    Your vote for Friday’s Radio Night, on the Kitchen Board from 12:00 to 20:00. One each; press another station to change it.

    /queue

    ◀️ Prev
    Previous page of the queue.
    ▶️ Next
    Next page of the queue.

    Kitchen Quiz

    ▶️ Start now Cancel
    Starts the quiz before its countdown runs out, or calls it off. Anyone in the quiz’s voice channel can press them.
    A · …
    Your guess. Four songs, one is playing. You have 20 seconds.
    🔁 Play again
    Starts another quiz from the podium.
