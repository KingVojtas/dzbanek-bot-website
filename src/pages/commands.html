    On the cards

    Buttons on the cards

    Now Playing

    Pause
    Pause, or Resume while playback is paused.
    Skip
    Skip to the next track in the queue.
    Stop
    Stop and leave. On the radio card, you need to be in the bot’s voice channel.
    Loop
    Cycles the label: Loop (off), Repeat (this track), Queue (the whole queue).
    Shuffle
    Shuffle the upcoming queue.

    Radio

    Website
    Opens the station’s site.
    Catch
    Saves the artist and title for you. A show name is refused — a show is not a song.
    Play
    On a caught song. Finds it on YouTube and queues it like /play. On the Kitchen Board, only the person who caught it can press Play.
