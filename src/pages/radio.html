Live radio in your voice channel

/radio play joins your voice channel and starts streaming. The card takes on the station’s color and updates when the song changes (it checks about every 20 seconds). It won’t leave just because the chat has gone quiet.

Catch a song, play it later

Hear something you like? Catch saves the artist and title and says so in the channel, with a Play button only you can press: it finds the song on YouTube and adds it to the queue. The Kitchen Board shows the server’s latest catch. While a station names only its show, Catch waits for a song.

Radio Night

/radio night sets up a Friday radio night (you need Manage Server). Pick a backup station and a voice channel, or just join one. /radio night-off cancels it.

Next Radio Night

  1. 12:00

    The Kitchen Board shows a vote button for each of the seven stations. Everyone gets one vote and can change it.

  2. While voting

    The board shows the votes as they come in. After 20:00 it goes back to normal.

  3. 20:00

    The winner starts playing in the channel you picked, and the board’s channel gets a note with the votes. A tie goes to the station you scheduled if it’s one of the tied ones, otherwise to the first of them in the /radio list.

Scheduled station

Switching stations keeps the same voice connection and replaces the old card. With /setup idle-radio (on by default), when the music queue runs out and people are still in the channel, the last station starts playing.
