Radio

/radio play joins your voice channel and streams Kiss, Rock Radio, or Radio Beat. The card uses the station’s color and shows the song on air, checking for a new one about every 20 seconds.

Catch and Play

Catch saves the song that’s on air. The reply has a Play button that finds the song on YouTube and adds it to the queue. The Kitchen Board shows the server’s latest catch.

Radio Night

/radio night schedules a station for Friday evening and needs Manage Server. You pick a fallback station and a voice channel. /radio night-off cancels it.

Next Radio Night:

12:00The Kitchen Board shows vote buttons for Kiss, Rock, and Beat. Everyone gets one vote and can change it.
During the voteThe board shows the votes so far. After 20:00 it goes back to showing what’s playing.
20:00The winning station starts in the chosen channel. On a tie it plays the scheduled station.

Switching stations keeps the same voice connection and replaces the old card. With /setup idle-radio, which is on by default, the last station starts playing when the music queue runs out.
