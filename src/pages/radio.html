Radio

Three Czech stations in your voice channel.

/radio play joins your voice channel and starts streaming. The card takes on the station’s color and updates when the song changes (it checks about every 20 seconds). It won’t leave just because the chat has gone quiet.

Catch

Catch a song, play it later

Hear something you like? Catch saves the artist and title. The reply comes with a Play button that finds the song on YouTube and adds it to the queue. The Kitchen Board shows the server’s latest catch.

Friday

Radio Night

/radio night sets up a Friday radio night (you need Manage Server). Pick a backup station and a voice channel, or just join one. /radio night-off cancels it.

Next Radio Night

  1. 12:00

    The Kitchen Board shows Kiss, Rock, and Beat buttons. Everyone gets one vote and can change it.

  2. While voting

    The board shows the votes as they come in. After 20:00 it goes back to normal.

  3. 20:00

    The winner starts playing in the channel you picked. If it’s a tie, it plays the station you scheduled, then the last one that played, then Beat.
