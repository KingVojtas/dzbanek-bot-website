The house rules

Tell it which shelf to use.

Every /setup subcommand needs Manage Server. Music and radio need no channel setup — only a voice channel you are in.

/setup steam

Where the 03:33 Steam digest is posted.

/setup epic

Where Epic free games are posted.

/setup welcome

Where join messages are posted.

/setup goodbye

Where leave messages are posted.

/setup kitchen

Where the living Kitchen Board hangs. This one is not guessed from a channel name.

/setup idle-radio

On by default. When the music queue ends, fade into the last radio station instead of leaving. Pass off to leave after the idle timeout.

/setup status

Shows this server’s channels, Radio Night, and idle radio.

/setup disable

Pause Steam, Epic, welcome, goodbye, the Kitchen Board, Radio Night, or idle radio.

Skip a step

Names it will find on its own

If you skip /setup for a feed, Dzbanek looks for a text channel whose name matches one of these.

The invite

What the invite asks for

Not Administrator. The invite bitfield is 3173440 — the permissions below, and nothing else.

3173440

Manage Messages is how Dzbanek deletes its own previous Now Playing card and the previous deal digest. It is not used to moderate people’s messages.

Server Members intent

Welcome and goodbye need the privileged Server Members intent turned on once for the bot application, in the Discord Developer Portal. People inviting the bot do not flip that switch. Someone self-hosting does.

Self-hosting, Spotify keys, and YouTube cookies are documented in the bot repository.
