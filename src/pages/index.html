Music

A queue, not a cluttered chat.

Drop a YouTube link or a search. Spotify tracks, albums, and playlists work too — Spotify is the recipe card, the audio comes from YouTube. Now Playing shows the art, a progress bar, and the transport buttons. When the next song starts, the old card is cleared.

  • Pause, skip, shuffle, and loop live on the card
  • The queue holds 100 tracks. /remove drops one by position
  • After 120 quiet seconds, the last radio station fades in if people are still there

Questions at the table

Before you ask.

Is it really free?

Yes. There is no premium tier and no locked commands. Invite it and the kitchen is yours.

Does it need Administrator?

No. The invite asks for Connect, Speak, View Channel, Send Messages, Embed Links, Manage Messages, and Add Reactions — nothing else. Manage Messages only clears the bot’s own old cards.

Why can’t music and radio play at once?

A bot gets one voice connection per server. Music and radio share it, so starting one hands the speaker over from the other.

Does this website show live stats?

No. The bot has no website API, so every card here is an example. The clock is real: it is your browser telling Prague time.

Can I host it myself?

Yes. It is a Node process with FFmpeg and Deno, on a home PC or a VPS. The repository has the full setup. GitHub

Put the kettle on.

The public bot uses the invite. The source, and how to self-host, is on GitHub.
