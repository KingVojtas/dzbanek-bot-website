Good to know

One thing at a time

Music and radio share one voice connection, so starting one stops the other. That’s a Discord limit.

Runs on Prague time

Deals, Radio Night, and the Sunday postcard follow Czech time, wherever your server is.

Lost? Type /help

It lists every command. Pause, skip, and loop are also buttons right on the card.

Actually free

No premium tier, nothing behind a paywall. Invite it and you get everything.

Music from YouTube and Spotify

Paste a YouTube link, a Spotify track or playlist, or just type a song name. Spotify links get matched on YouTube, since that’s where the audio comes from. The Now Playing card shows the cover, the progress, and the controls, and the old card disappears when the next song starts, so your channel doesn’t fill up with junk.

  • Pause, skip, shuffle, and loop are right on the card
  • Up to 100 songs in the queue. /remove takes one out
  • Queue ran out but people are still hanging around? After two minutes it switches to the last radio station
  • Kitchen Quiz: /quiz start plays short clips of songs your kitchen has played, and everyone in voice races to name them

Seven stations, live

/radio play joins your voice channel and streams one of seven stations: Kiss, Rock Radio, Radio Beat and Frekvence 1 from Czechia, Heart and Capital from London, and Radio Paradise from California. The card shows what’s on and updates when the song changes. Hear something good? Hit Catch to save it, then Play to queue it from YouTube.

One message that edits itself

/setup kitchen posts one message with four cards and keeps editing it instead of posting new ones: what’s playing, who’s in voice, the top Steam deal with the Epic freebie, and a last card with the latest caught song, how many people joined today, and Radio Night.

  • The In voice card lists every call in the server, even when the bot isn’t in one, with who’s muted, deafened, streaming, or on camera. A call shows only if everyone who can read the board can see that channel, so private calls stay private.
  • Every Sunday at 18:00 you also get a postcard with the songs people caught that week, plus Friday’s vote results if anyone voted.
  • The bot’s Discord status takes turns with three lines, each for twenty minutes: Purifying the source, Analyzing bacteria levels and Pouring fresh drops.

  • It tags the person, so they get a ping.

  • The welcome says when they joined Discord, the goodbye says when they first sat down.

Setting it up

  1. Invite it

    Click Add to Server and accept the permissions. Music works right away if you’re in a voice channel.

    Add to Server

  2. Pick some channels

    Optional. Admins can run /setup to choose where deals, welcome messages, and the Kitchen Board go. Channels with obvious names get found on their own.

  3. Play something

    Hop into voice and run /play, or /radio play and pick a station.

Why Dzbanek exists

I made Dzbanek for our own Discord server. We wanted one bot for music instead of three, the Czech radio we listen to anyway, and a heads-up when a good game gets cheap.

It’s free and it’ll stay that way. If something breaks or you miss a feature, tell me on GitHub.

Things people ask me

Is it really free?

Yep. No premium tier, nothing locked. Invite it and you get everything.

Does it need admin rights?

No. It asks for Connect, Speak, View Channel, Send Messages, Embed Links, Manage Messages, and Add Reactions, and that’s it. Manage Messages is only for deleting its own old cards.

Why can’t music and radio play at the same time?

Discord gives a bot one voice connection per server, so music and radio take turns.

Is anything on this site live?

The clock and the radio. The radio plays the real stations, and the song info comes from each station’s own feed: radia.cz, Triton, Global, and Radio Paradise’s stream. The bot itself has no public API, so the Discord cards here are examples.

Can I host it myself?

Sure. It’s a Node.js app that needs FFmpeg and Deno, and it runs fine on a home PC or a VPS. The repo has the full setup guide. GitHub
