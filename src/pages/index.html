The basics

Good to know before you invite it.

One thing at a time

Music and radio share one voice connection, so starting one stops the other. That’s a Discord limit.

Runs on Prague time

Deals, Radio Night, and the Sunday postcard follow Czech time, wherever your server is.

Six commands

/play, /queue, /stop, /remove, /radio, and /setup. Everything else is a button.

Actually free

No premium tier, nothing behind a paywall. Invite it and you get everything.

Music

Play anything, keep the chat clean.

Paste a YouTube link, a Spotify track or playlist, or just type a song name. Spotify links get matched on YouTube, since that’s where the audio comes from. The Now Playing card shows the cover, the progress, and the controls, and the old card disappears when the next song starts, so your channel doesn’t fill up with junk.

  • Pause, skip, shuffle, and loop are right on the card
  • Up to 100 songs in the queue. /remove takes one out
  • Queue ran out but people are still hanging around? After two minutes it switches to the last radio station

Kitchen Board

One message that keeps itself up to date.

/setup kitchen posts a single message and keeps editing it instead of posting new ones. It shows what’s playing, who’s in voice, the top Steam deal, the current Epic freebie, how many people joined today, and Radio Night if one’s coming up.

  • Every Sunday at 18:00 you also get a postcard with the songs people caught that week, plus Friday’s vote results if anyone voted.
  • The bot’s Discord status shows what’s going on in its busiest server: radio first, then music, then the top Steam deal. When nothing’s on, it just says the kitchen is quiet.

Getting started

Up and running in three steps.

  1. 1

    Invite it

    Click Add to Server and accept the permissions. Music works right away if you’re in a voice channel.

  2. 2

    Pick some channels

    Optional. Admins can run /setup to choose where deals, welcome messages, and the Kitchen Board go. Channels with obvious names get found on their own.

  3. 3

    Play something

    Hop into voice and run /play, or /radio play for Kiss, Rock, or Beat.

FAQ

Questions people ask.

Is it really free?

Yep. No premium tier, nothing locked. Invite it and you get everything.

Does it need admin rights?

No. It asks for Connect, Speak, View Channel, Send Messages, Embed Links, Manage Messages, and Add Reactions, and that’s it. Manage Messages is only for deleting its own old cards.

Why can’t music and radio play at the same time?

Discord gives a bot one voice connection per server, so music and radio take turns.

Is anything on this site live?

Just the clock. The bot doesn’t have a public API, so all the cards here are examples. The clock is your browser showing Prague time.

Can I host it myself?

Sure. It’s a Node.js app that needs FFmpeg and Deno, and it runs fine on a home PC or a VPS. The repo has the full setup guide. GitHub
