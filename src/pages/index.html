Dzbanek

A Discord bot that plays music, streams Czech radio, and posts Steam deals and Epic free games. It’s free and has six slash commands.

Add to Discord See the commands

Good to know

One voice connection
Music and radio share the bot’s voice connection in a server. Starting one stops the other.
Prague time
Deals, Radio Night, and the Sunday postcard run on Europe/Prague time.
Six slash commands
/play, /queue, /stop, /remove, /radio, and /setup. Everything else is a button on the cards.
Free
No premium tier and nothing locked.

Music

/play takes a YouTube link, a Spotify track, album, or playlist, or just a search. Spotify links are matched to the same song on YouTube, which is where the audio comes from.

The Now Playing card shows the cover, a progress bar, and the controls. When the next song starts, the previous card is deleted, so the channel doesn’t fill up with old cards.

  • Pause, skip, shuffle, and loop are buttons on the card.
  • The queue holds up to 100 tracks. /remove takes one out by its position.
  • If the queue runs out while people are still in the channel, the last radio station starts after 120 seconds.

Radio

/radio play joins your voice channel and streams one of three Czech stations. The card shows the song that’s on air and updates when it changes. Catch saves that song for you, and Play queues it from YouTube.

Steam and Epic

Steam daily deals

Every day at 03:33, ten games on sale. Games rated Very Positive come first, sorted by discount. If there aren’t ten of them, other current Steam specials fill the list. Prices are in euros.

Epic free games

Checked at 12:00 and 17:00, around when Epic changes its weekly free games. Each server gets each lineup once.

Kitchen Board

/setup kitchen posts one message and keeps editing it instead of posting new ones. It shows what’s playing, who’s in voice, the top Steam deal, the current Epic free game, how many people joined today, and Radio Night when one is scheduled.

On Sundays at 18:00 the channel also gets a postcard with the songs people caught that week, plus Friday’s vote results if there was a vote.

The bot’s Discord status shows what’s happening in its busiest server: radio first, then music, then the top Steam deal.

Getting started

  1. Add the bot

    Use the invite link and accept the permissions. Music works right away in any voice channel you’re in.

  2. Pick channels

    Optional. Admins run /setup to choose where deals, welcome messages, and the Kitchen Board go. Channels with common names are found automatically.

  3. Play something

    Join a voice channel and run /play, or /radio play for Kiss, Rock Radio, or Radio Beat.

FAQ

Is it free?

Yes. There’s no premium tier and nothing is locked.

Does it need Administrator?

No. It asks for Connect, Speak, View Channel, Send Messages, Embed Links, Manage Messages, and Add Reactions. Manage Messages is only used to delete its own old cards.

Why can’t music and radio play at the same time?

A bot gets one voice connection per server, so music and radio share it.

Does this site show live data?

No. The bot has no public API, so the cards on this site are examples. The clock is real: it’s your browser showing Prague time.

Can I host it myself?

Yes. It’s a Node.js app that needs FFmpeg and Deno, and it runs on a home PC or a VPS. Setup instructions are in the repository. GitHub
