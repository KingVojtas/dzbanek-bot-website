House rules

Dzbanek is a free bot I make in my spare time. Adding it to a server or using its commands means you’re fine with these few rules. They’re short on purpose.

Free, as it is
Dzbanek costs nothing, and it comes as it is. I fix what I can, but I can’t promise it’s always online or always right, and one day it may change or stop. As far as the law allows, I’m not responsible for what happens because you used it.
Use it kindly
Don’t use it to break Discord’s Terms of Service, its Community Guidelines, or the law. Don’t try to crash it, flood it, or get around its limits.
Your server
Admins choose where it posts with /setup, can switch things off with /setup disable, and can kick it any time. What people play through it is up to them.
Music and radio
Songs come from YouTube (Spotify links are matched there), and the radio from each station’s public stream. They belong to their owners. The bot plays them in your voice channel and doesn’t keep them.
Deals
Prices and free games come from Steam and Epic and can change at any time. Check the store before you buy.
Your data
What the bot keeps, and how to have it removed, is all on the privacy page. Privacy
On its own
Dzbanek isn’t made by or connected to Discord, YouTube, Spotify, Valve, Epic Games, or any of the radio stations.
Your own copy
These rules are about the public bot. If you run your own copy from the code on GitHub, you run it yourself.
Changes
If these rules change, the new version goes up here with a new date. Using the bot after that means you’re fine with it.

A question about the rules?

Write to me on GitHub. These rules apply from 27 October 2026.
